The Lab — Build a Flink Pipeline

Everything from Modules 03–06 in one place: build a real pipeline out of filter, keyBy, and windowed-aggregate operators, then watch the same fixed orders stream from Module 06 flow through it, one event at a time, in real time.

How this works: pick a mission below (or go freeform in Sandbox mode), build your pipeline from the operator palette, then step through the stream and watch each operator card update live. Nothing here is faked — the same small engine that renders the pipeline also computes the real aggregate results, so what you see is what your pipeline actually does.
