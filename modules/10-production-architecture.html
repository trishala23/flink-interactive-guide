Module 10 · Capstone

Production Architecture Lab

Every previous module touched one piece of a real system. This lab assembles a full, realistic production stack out of those pieces — the kind of architecture you'd actually find behind a Flink job running in AWS — and lets you click through every layer and watch a record flow through it.

The stack

LayerTechnology
RuntimeApache Flink 1.20.1 (streaming)
LanguageJava 11
BuildMaven + Shade plugin (fat JAR)
MessagingKafka (multiple topics) + AWS Kinesis
StorageAWS DynamoDB
Archival / DLQAWS Kinesis Firehose
TracingDatadog APM (dd-java-agent)
MetricsFlink metrics + StatsD protocol
SerializationProtobuf
DeployAWS Managed Service for Apache Flink (MSF/KDA)

Nothing here is a new Flink concept on its own — it's Module 09's sources, Module 03's process functions and side outputs, Module 05's state, and Module 07's managed-deployment row, wired together the way a real team actually wires them.
